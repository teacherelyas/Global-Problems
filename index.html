Lesson 25 – Global Problems

Major Global Problems

Today, we face a number of serious global problems that affect people, animals, and the environment. Some of these problems are closely connected, meaning that one problem can make another problem worse.

Section 1

Environmental Problems

One of the most critical global issues is preserving and protecting the environment. For many years, humans have depended heavily on fossil fuels, such as oil, coal, and natural gas. However, burning fossil fuels produces greenhouse gases, which trap heat in the atmosphere and contribute to global warming and climate change.

Climate change refers to long-term changes in the Earth's temperature and weather patterns. As global temperatures rise, many regions experience changes in rainfall, rising sea levels, and more extreme weather events.

Section 1 (continued)

The Cost of Environmental Damage

Some scientists believe that humans have already caused irreparable damage to the planet. In other words, some environmental damage may be impossible to completely reverse. We are already seeing serious consequences, including rising sea levels and increasingly frequent natural disasters such as hurricanes and tornadoes.

The natural world is also suffering. Some animal species have become extinct, while many others are endangered and at risk of disappearing. To address these problems, countries need to invest in clean energy and renewable resources such as solar, wind, and water power. Governments and companies should also develop manufacturing methods that do not harm the environment.

Section 2

Poverty and Hunger

Environmental problems are not the only major challenges facing the world. Extreme poverty is still common in many developing countries, particularly in rural areas.

In some regions, children die of hunger or starvation because they do not have enough food. Many communities also experience serious food shortages and lack access to clean and safe drinking water.

Section 2 (continued)

Poverty in Cities and Society

Poverty can affect people's lives in cities as well as rural areas. When there are not enough homeless shelters, homeless people may have no choice but to sleep on the streets.

There are also many families living on the margins of society. These families may be socially or economically disadvantaged and may live below the poverty line. As a result, they often struggle to make ends meet, meaning that they have difficulty paying for basic necessities such as food, housing, and healthcare.

Many governments have created welfare programs to help people in difficult financial situations and to alleviate poverty. However, the growing gap between rich and poor suggests that completely eradicating poverty may be extremely difficult.

Section 3

Problems Connected to Poverty

Poverty and underdevelopment can contribute to many other serious problems. For example, people in poor communities may die from preventable diseases because they do not have access to safe and effective vaccines or medical treatment.

Poverty can also contribute to child labor. Some large corporations have been criticized for using children to manufacture products. In extremely poor families, parents may feel that they have no other option but to send their children to work so that the family can survive.

Section 3 (continued)

Human Trafficking

Another serious problem is human trafficking. Young women from disadvantaged backgrounds can be particularly vulnerable to trafficking and sexual exploitation because they may have limited financial opportunities and support.

Section 4

A Connected Global Problem

These problems are not separate from one another. Climate change can affect agriculture, which can lead to food shortages. Food shortages can increase poverty, while poverty can make people more vulnerable to disease, exploitation, and other social problems.

For this reason, solving global problems requires cooperation between governments, businesses, scientists, and ordinary citizens. Protecting the environment, reducing poverty, improving healthcare, and creating better economic opportunities are all important parts of building a healthier and more sustainable future.
